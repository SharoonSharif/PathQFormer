# Venue options (free to publish, strong topic fit)

Checked on the journals' own pages on 2026-10-16 (speed figures, fees, author rules) and 2026-10-19 (e-mail rules).
Elsevier publishes medians without stating the period they cover; their first-decision medians are mostly desk
decisions, so the table ranks on the time to a decision after review. "Free" means a route with no
article-processing charge and no mandatory page charge at this paper's length; every journal below is hybrid, so
the free route leaves the published version behind a paywall (an arXiv preprint is allowed everywhere listed).

Each journal above published histology plus genomics survival work on TCGA, or missing-modality fusion, in 2025-2026.

## What each would need

- **JBHI**: nothing; the package in this folder is ready (8 + 5 pages). The e-mail rule must be settled with the
  editorial office first (see `CHECKLIST.md`).
- **TCBB**: IEEE Computer Society template (may add about a page), abstract cut to at most 200 words without
  citations, a short biography per author (counts toward the 12 pages), supplement as a separate file, cover letter
  that leads with the RNA pathway side ("a genuine connection to biology" is required). ORCID for every author.
- **Computerized Medical Imaging and Graphics**: Elsevier template; no length limit; single-anonymized review.
- **Journal of Biomedical Informatics**: structured abstract (Objective / Methods / Results / Conclusion, at most
  300 words), body at most 6,000 words, at most 8 figures plus tables, a Statement of Significance table and a
  graphical abstract; frame the work as multimodal informatics, not image analysis.
- **Expert Systems with Applications**: double-anonymized review (anonymize the repository links), author-year
  references; about 11% acceptance.
- **Neurocomputing**: author biographies with photographs; the guide rejects "pure combination of existing
  algorithms", so present the audit and recipe as a learning-systems contribution.
- **Medical Image Analysis**: one attempt only; a mandatory Statement of Significance asks for methodological
  novelty and whole-slide figures.
- **Pattern Recognition**: single-column, 20-35 pages. **Information Fusion**: at most 50 references (the paper has 59).

## Ruled out

- Computers in Biology and Medicine: Web of Science coverage put on hold in 2024.
- IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging: no supplements of this kind, 10-page submission cap, charges past 8 pages.
- Modern Pathology: requires an advance in diagnostic pathology.
- Bioinformatics, Briefings in Bioinformatics, BMC Bioinformatics, Scientific Reports, PLOS Computational Biology:
  fully open access with an article-processing charge.
- Computer Methods and Programs in Biomedicine (3,500-word cap) and JCO Clinical Cancer Informatics (3,000-word cap):
  free, but the paper would need large cuts.
- TMLR: free to publish and read, but a second submission so soon after the September 2026 desk rejection risks
  another one.
